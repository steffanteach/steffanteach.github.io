Payments & Booking

Simple, secure payment options and an easy booking process to get started.

How to Book

Getting started is straightforward. Follow these simple steps to schedule your first session.

  • 01

    Get in Touch

    Email me at steffdjones@gmail.com with your needs, goals, and availability. We'll schedule a free 15-minute consultation to discuss how I can help.

  • 02

    Choose Your Service

    Decide whether you need academic tutoring, university guidance, or both. We'll create a customized plan based on your specific requirements.

  • 03

    Make Payment

    Secure your booking with payment via bank transfer, PayPal, or card payment. You'll receive confirmation and scheduling details immediately.

  • 04

    Start Learning

    Show up prepared for your first session. All materials and resources will be provided as needed.

Policies

Referral Discount

Save 15% and help others! When you refer someone and they book a session mentioning your name, you both receive 15% off your next session or package. There's no limit to how many referrals you can make.

In-Person vs Online Sessions

Online: Available to all UK students. No travel fees.

In-Person: Available within 45 miles of Neath. Travel fees: £0 (0-5mi), £5 (5-15mi), £10 (15-30mi), £15 (30-45mi). To book in-person, email your postcode and I'll confirm your travel fee.

Cancellation Policy

Sessions can be cancelled or rescheduled with at least 24 hours notice at no charge. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice will forfeit that session.

Refund Policy

Single sessions are non-refundable once booked. For packages, unused sessions may be refunded within the first month of purchase, minus any sessions already completed.

Package Expiration

Session packages (5 or 10 sessions) must be used within 3 months of purchase. Extensions may be granted in exceptional circumstances.

Session Format

Online Sessions: Conducted via video call (Zoom, Google Meet, etc.). Available to students across the UK.

In-Person Sessions: Available within 45 miles of Neath, Wales. Travel fees apply: 0-5 miles (free), 5-15 miles (+£5), 15-30 miles (+£10), 30-45 miles (+£15). Sessions typically held at your home, local library, or another agreed location.

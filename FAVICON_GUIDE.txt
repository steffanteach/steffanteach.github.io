FAVICON CREATION GUIDE
=====================

Option 1: Use a Free Online Tool
---------------------------------
1. Go to https://favicon.io/
2. Choose "Text" and enter "SJ" (your initials)
3. Select a simple font and black/white colors
4. Download the favicon
5. Rename to favicon.png
6. Place in assets/ folder

Option 2: Use Canva (Free)
---------------------------
1. Go to canva.com
2. Create 512x512px design
3. Add text "SJ" or your initials
4. Download as PNG
5. Rename to favicon.png
6. Place in assets/ folder

Option 3: Use Your Profile Photo
---------------------------------
1. Crop profile photo to square
2. Resize to 512x512px
3. Save as favicon.png
4. Place in assets/ folder

Quick & Simple: Just use "SJ" initials on white background!

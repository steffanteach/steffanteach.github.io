About Steffan

My journey from a small town in Wales to Harvard wasn't straightforward, but it taught me something invaluable: with the right guidance and determination, anything is possible.

Growing up in Neath, I was the first in my family to attend university. I know what it's like to navigate the complex world of higher education without a roadmap. That's why I'm passionate about helping students—especially those from similar backgrounds—reach their full potential.

At Harvard, I didn't just earn my degree in Mechanical Engineering. I became deeply involved in the admissions process, conducting interviews for prospective students. I've also helped students prepare for MIT and Oxford interviews, and work hands-on with the Seren Network—Wales' programme supporting students aiming for top universities. Additionally, I mentor MIT students during their study abroad experiences.

Whether you're struggling with a specific subject, preparing for crucial exams, or dreaming of studying at a top university, I understand the process from both sides. Being bilingual in English and Welsh means I can support students in the language they're most comfortable with.

Frequently Asked Questions

What subjects can you help with?

I can tutor any subject at any level—from primary school through A-Levels and beyond. Just provide me with the syllabus or worksheets, and I'll prepare tailored lessons. My specialties include Mathematics, Sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Engineering, Computer Science, and English/Welsh language and literature.

How do online sessions work?

Online sessions are conducted via video call (Zoom, Google Meet, or your preferred platform). I use a digital whiteboard for explaining concepts, and you can share your screen to show me work you're struggling with. It's just as effective as in-person tutoring!

Do you offer in-person tutoring?

Yes! I offer in-person tutoring within 45 miles of Neath, Wales. Travel fees apply based on distance: 0-5 miles (free), 5-15 miles (+£5), 15-30 miles (+£10), 30-45 miles (+£15). Sessions can be at your home, a local library, or another agreed location.

How do I know if you're the right tutor for me?

Great question! I offer a free 15-minute consultation where we can discuss your goals, challenges, and learning style. This gives us both a chance to see if we're a good fit before committing to any sessions.

Can you help with both UK and US university applications?

Absolutely. Having gone through the US application process myself (and helped numerous students with UK applications), I'm familiar with both systems. I can guide you through UCAS, Common App, Oxbridge interviews, supplemental essays, and more.

What's your cancellation policy?

Sessions can be cancelled or rescheduled with at least 24 hours notice at no charge. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice will forfeit that session. I understand life happens—just let me know as soon as possible!

How does the referral discount work?

When someone books a session and mentions your name, you both receive 15% off your next session. There's no limit to how many people you can refer—share the success and save!

Do you provide tutoring in Welsh?

Yes! I'm fluent in both English and Welsh, and I'm happy to conduct sessions in whichever language you're most comfortable with. This applies to all subjects and services.

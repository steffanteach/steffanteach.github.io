Application Timeline

Success in university admissions requires careful planning. Here's a typical timeline for UK applications:

  • Year 12 Summer

    Research & Planning

    Begin researching universities, attend open days, identify potential courses and start thinking about your personal statement.

  • Sept - Oct

    Application Preparation

    Draft personal statement, gather references, complete UCAS form. Oxbridge applicants must submit by mid-October.

  • Nov - Dec

    Final Submissions

    Standard UCAS deadline in mid-January. Oxbridge interviews typically held in December.

  • Jan - March

    Interviews & Offers

    University interviews for competitive courses. Begin receiving offers and making decisions.

  • May - August

    Final Decisions

    Confirm choices, take final exams, receive final offers, and prepare for university life.

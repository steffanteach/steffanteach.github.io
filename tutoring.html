My Approach

I believe in teaching concepts, not just answers. My goal is to help you develop critical thinking skills and problem-solving strategies that extend beyond any single subject or exam.

Each session is tailored to your specific needs, whether you're struggling with a particular concept, preparing for exams, or looking to advance beyond your current curriculum.

  • 01. Assessment We start by identifying your current level, learning style, and specific goals.
  • 02. Custom Plan I create a personalized learning plan targeting your areas of need.
  • 03. Active Learning Sessions focus on hands-on problem solving and conceptual understanding.
  • 04. Progress Tracking Regular check-ins ensure we're meeting your goals and adjusting as needed.
